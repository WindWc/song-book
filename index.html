Paste a song, any way you have it

Chords on the line above the lyrics, or inline like [G]Son of the wind. Put section names on their own line. To repeat a section, write just its name again. Chorus x2 plays it twice. Notes like (Finger pick) or *Strum* show up but aren’t counted. (4 beats) above a line makes just that line 4 beats long. Set the capo with Capo: 3 or the picker. Your beat timing stays put when you edit text.
